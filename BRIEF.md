## 3. The games

### 3.1 Individual betting — your 5 bets ($50)
5 bets × $10, your own picks, any of the 12 races.
**No double-dipping:** can't bet a race you're repping the group in (your Pick of the Day race, or your quaddie leg) — app greys it out.

### 3.2 Pick of the Day — your 6th bet ($25) · DOUBLE points
App **randomly assigns** each person one of **8 nominated races** (Flem R4–9 + Rose R9, R10); you pick the horse.
Every race ends up with a **boy's pick vs a girl's pick** → feeds Girls-vs-Guys.

### 3.3 Boys' Flemington Quaddie — R6–9 ($125)
App **randomly splits the 8 boys 4+4**: one group covers the first 2 legs (R6, R7), the other the last 2 (R8, R9).
Random part = *which people* land where. Each player then **chooses their own horse in both their legs** (~up to 4 numbers/leg before double-ups). Points per correct leg; lands if all 4 legs covered & won.

### 3.4 Girls' Flemington Quaddie — R6–9 ($125)
Same mechanic, 8 girls.

### 3.5 Rosehill Mixed Quaddie — R7–10 ($200)
**All 16** players enter their own number for each leg in the app; it **auto-populates** the quaddie line.
Double-ups expected → ~4–7 numbers/leg. Points per correct leg. (Loaded bigger because more combinations.)

### 3.6 Steaks Day Feature 🥩 — Flem R8 Champions Stakes ($200) · TRIPLE points
Boys **team-vote** one horse, Girls **team-vote** one horse (most-voted runs).
**$100 each-way per team** ($50 win + $50 place). 🥂 "Raise Your Glasses" toast + countdown before the jump.
The marquee Girls-vs-Guys duel.

### 3.7 Get Out of Jail — last race each track ($50)
Boys on **Flem R9**, Girls on **Rose R10**. $25 each. Final redemption bet.

### 3.8 Mysteries — the wheel 🎡 ($100)
4 team bets, $25 each: Boys-Melb, Boys-Syd, Girls-Melb, Girls-Syd.
Two-stage spin: **spin 1 = random race** (that city), **spin 2 = random horse**. Feeds Girls-vs-Guys.

### 3.9 Mug Punter 🥄
Live wooden-spoon badge on whoever's last on the leaderboard. Locked in at day's end.

## 4. Scoring & leaderboard
- **Points = your return/winnings** (back winners at good odds → climb). Multipliers: **Pick of the Day ×2**, **Steaks Day Feature ×3**.
- **Two streams:** personal bets (your 6) → Individual/Couple boards. Team games (quaddies, feature, mysteries, get-out) → **Girls vs Guys** tally.
- **Three live views** off the same numbers: Individual (16) / Couple (8) / 👗 Girls vs Guys 👔.
- 🥄 Mug = lowest. Group P&L (real Sportsbet $2000) shown up top.

## 6. Tech
